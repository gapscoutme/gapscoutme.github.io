GapScout

A market-research tool for one person.

What it is

GapScout is a private, single-user application. You type in a broad market, and it narrows that market to a niche, proposes a product and a price, estimates the share of customers the product could win, works out what acquiring those customers would cost, projects five years of revenue and profit, and reports a confidence level that the niche is viable.

How it works

Every number in the result is drawn from a named source, dated, and shown with a range. The sources are free, public, official datasets: United States Census business statistics, Bureau of Labor Statistics wage data, government procurement records, state licensing files, and similar. Where the tool uses Google Ads data, it reads keyword search-volume and cost-per-click estimates for the operator's own Google Ads account; it never creates, changes, or runs advertising campaigns.

Who uses it

GapScout is run by its developer for his own market research. It is not offered to the public, has no user accounts, and is not for sale.
